So I did NOT find a way to beat Teero here. My best search plays this section ~13 ticks slower than his
official time from a state taken from his own track. What I could establish (details below):

* The maze has exactly one route (sec. 1). Every part of it is a tight turn in a 3-8 tile corridor lined
  with freeze; the speed limit is set by turning, not by the ramp: hook-only turning radius is v^2/3 px
  (|v| <= ~17-28 px/t for the 96-256 px corridors), so |v| stays at 18-30 px/t for the whole section and
  each 25-tick reload buys one 12 px/t kick. Geodesic progress: Teero 3304 px in 149 ticks (22.2 px/t),
  our best 20.4 px/t.
* Locally the search is at par with Teero: started from Teero's own (track-estimated) states it reproduces
  his segment times within the track noise (k2419 -> top passage x>8900: 2447 vs his 2445; k2452 -> under
  the island: 2477-2478 vs 2478; k2520 -> finish: 2543 vs 2543 implied by his own track, see sec. 2.4).
  The 13 ticks come from accumulating ~1-3 ticks per turn from our own (slightly worse) states: the up-turn
  into the column (we enter at vy -30, his track says -33..-35) -> corner +2 -> ceiling kick 6 ticks later
  than his (#47 k2435.8 vs our k2442) -> +3 at the top passage, +4..+7 after the right-side U-turn, +13 at
  the finish (table in sec. 4).
* Teero's track is not self-consistent at the very end: simulating from his track state at k2536 (or k2520)
  gives the finish at k2543 (needs >= 20-25 px more x than his last track points). Either the track lags
  ~3-4 ticks in the final room or it is ~25 px off in x there. Per-gate comparisons near the end carry this
  +-3 tick uncertainty; the bottom-line comparison uses his official time.
* Entry-state sensitivity is small (sec. 5): +-5 px/t of entry speed, +-30 px of height, reload phase 0..10
  and the air jump change the column arrival by <= 1 tick. The up-turn always lands on the bare platform,
  ground-jumps and hooks the hill; what matters for later is the RELOAD PHASE (sec. 3.3) and keeping energy
  through the up-turn.
* seg's failure here had two causes (sec. 6): a teleport clock bug (gate=finish can never fire when
  tpk > prefix length) and a beam collapse at the up-turn (no positional diversity; shot children with
  energy/speed credit fill the beam and all crash). segx/seg.cpp (patched copy) fixes both and reaches the
  finish (2581); fbeam (new tool) reaches 2552.

=========================================================================================================
3. PHYSICS / STRUCTURE (what limits the speed)
=========================================================================================================
3.1 Turning, not the ramp. The ramp only caps HORIZONTAL displacement (vertical is free), but nowhere in this
    maze can the tee use |v| > ~35: every leg ends in a turn of 90-180 deg inside 96-256 px. Above 15 px/t
    the hook only acts when the new |v| (after gravity) is not larger than the old one, so it can only brake
    or rotate (rotation <= 3/|v| rad/tick, radius >= |v|^2/3 px). Kicks (12 px/t, one per 25 ticks; two at
    once with a pre-fire) and wall/floor contacts (which zero the normal component) are the only sharper
    turns. Gravity 0.5/t; the hook's downward pull is *0.3, so vertical braking while rising is <= 1.4/t^2.
3.2 Energy. E = v^2 - y is conserved in free flight. The entry has E ~ +150 (|v| 64): ~3000 units are
    destroyed in the up-turn (the 110 deg turn into a 96 px column has ~10 ticks between the platform and the
    column; the hook can lift only while the motion is still mostly horizontal, sec. 3.4). After that both
    Teero and we fly at |v| 18-30 and E -2800..-3900; kicks along the motion are the energy source
    (+2*v*12 per kick). Our best run's energy is at or above Teero's from k2445 on - the deficit is position
    (timing), not speed.
3.3 Reload clock. With 25-tick reload there are ~6 kicks in the section; every turn wants one. An idle loaded
    grenade is a lost kick; a pre-fire (shot that explodes n ticks later) starts the reload n ticks earlier
    for the same kick. Example (scanfire on our line): firing at k2410 with aim -48 deg explodes at k2418 on
    the column's right wall (8608,4070) and gives the same kick as our point-blank shot at k2416, with the
    reload back 6 ticks earlier. In our lines that alone did not help (2553 with the forced pre-fire,
    runs/f6pf): we reach the ceiling only at k2441 anyway because our climb is slower.
3.4 The up-turn (k2390 -> column). Best found (all entry variants): hook the hill's lower edge early
    (k~2392-2402), land on the bare platform (k2403, vy -> 0, jumps refilled), ground jump (k2404-2405:
    vy = -13.2, plus up to -3 from the hook), hold the hill hook while braking vx (~4/tick), kick off the
    column's right wall (aim 15-25 deg down-right, explosion at x=8608 behind the freeze col 268, kick
    ~(-12,-8)) at k2414-2417 -> column at vy ~ -30, gate "column y<3900" at k2420-2421.
    Why not more: above 15 px/t a hook pulls up only while its pull also brakes enough; with v=(40,-16) the
    anchor may be at most ~57 deg above the backward horizontal (pull (-1.3,-2.5)/t), with v=(25,-23) at most
    ~35 deg. As the velocity turns upward the hook can only brake. Variants tried and not better: steeper
    re-hooks at k2405-2412 (all aims -175..-115 deg, dir -1/0/+1; best equal to ours), jump + platform kick
    (k2406: vy -27 but vx 43 left -> column at k2428), finer hook aims (128-256 aims), longer rollouts,
    hill climbing (fhc, 400k evaluations, +0.3 tick).
3.5 Top turn (column -> top passage). Rise past the corner (8576,3744) at -25..-30, hook down-right on the
    island's left face / top (anchor ~(8608-8650,3776-3848)) to build vx +1..2/tick (dir +1), reach the
    ceiling band y in [3488,3503] (the only place a ceiling kick has full strength: solid at y<=3455, centre
    must stay >= 3488) and kick up-left (aim -105..-127 deg) -> v ~(25,-2). Then cruise at vx ~24 (dir +1,
    hook pulses on the island top). Cutting the corner low is slower (vx 14-21 afterwards).
3.6 Right side U-turn: swing down around the island's right end (hooks on the island top/right end, anchor
    ~(9125-9180,3776)), drop to y~3900, go left under the island at -15..-20, kick from below the island
    (aim up) at k~2478, down through the hole at x~9000, hook the lower chamber's ceiling/hole edge, right at
    +20 to the gap.
3.7 Final room: kick off the gap's right wall (k2519, aim right -> kick left, vx -23), floor touch + jumps to
    stay in the room's upper half at vx -25, hook the island top (anchor (8880-9000,4512)) to brake while
    dropping into the left column, reverse vx with the hook at (8880,4512), kick (k2545, aim up-right at the
    island tip's underside -> kick down-left, vy +9) and hook right to the finish.

=========================================================================================================
6. SEARCH: WHY seg DIED, WHAT WORKS (segx/seg.cpp, fbeam.cpp)
=========================================================================================================
6.1 seg tp bug (all tp runs with gate=finish): seg sets m_StartTick = m_Tick - tpk after the prefix. With
    e1025.txt (1095 inputs) and tpk=2390 this is negative, and CTasGame::CheckRace only records a finish when
    m_StartTick >= 0 -> gate=finish can never fire (NOGATE for every tp job in runs/tpF). Fix (segx, fbeam):
    advance the world clock before setting the race clock (m_Tick += 4000; no projectile exists at that
    point; verified identical physics incl. shots with fl trace FL_NOSHIFT=1 vs default).
6.2 seg beam collapse at the up-turn (tested with the patched seg, beam 4000, the tpF f7 settings):
    default -> beam 1056 at step 20, NOGATE; survevery=0 -> 5 states at step 20, NOGATE (so survival pruning
    is not the cause); kcredit=0 -> NOGATE; ghost=1 or ghost=0 -> dies even earlier; fire=0 -> survives and
    finishes at 2593. Cause: without positional diversity the beam fills with the states the time model likes
    most - fast states and freshly kicked ones (judged after their explosion) moving at 40-60 px/t into the
    valley - none of which can make the 110 deg turn into the 3-tile column; survival rollouts (constant dir,
    hook keep/release/new hook, no kicks/jumps) cannot tell recoverable states apart and are switched off
    when all are doomed.
    Fix in segx: `quota=Q` (new, default 0 = old behaviour): first pass keeps at most Q states per coarse
    cell (x/32, y/32, vx/6, vy/6, loaded, shot in flight, air jump), second pass fills the beam with the best
    of the rest. quota=6 with the f7 settings: survives, finish 2581.
    Usage: physlab4/segx/seg AiP-Gores.map prefix=runs/ex/e1025.txt ref=teero_track.txt gate=finish
      horizon=150 threads=1 tp=7817,4000,60.7,21.6 tpk=2390 tpreload=3 beam=4000 survevery=2 survive=30
      ghost=3 hnow=600 ghoste=0.0 sinks=1156,1285,1443,1793,2143,2465 brake=2 kcredit=2 kready=10 rothook=1
      quant=1 maxticks=250 quota=6 out=...      (run from tas-work)
6.3 fbeam (new, physlab4/fbeam.cpp; build: ./build.sh fbeam): beam to the finish from a teleported entry.
    - Progress = geodesic distance D to the finish tiles (4-px field: box vs solid, centre vs freeze),
      turned into time by Teero's own remaining time at that D (monotone envelope; model=1). model=0 (D/v0),
      model=2 (Teero's arc position) and model=3 (mix) were tried: not better.
    - Score = t + n + T(D(end of rollout)) over 3-6 core-only rollouts of tau ticks (dir -1/0/1 x hook kept /
      released); a rollout that touches freeze pays deathpen*(1-n/2tau); rollouts may take one virtual
      point-blank kick (24 directions, explosion strength by distance) instead of dying if the grenade is
      loaded by then (vkick=1).
    - Shots: 48 aims prescreened analytically (CProjectile path); every child with a grenade in flight that
      explodes within firelook ticks is judged after its explosion (holding its input). Without this,
      pre-fired branches are scored as "reload spent, no kick" and die before their kick lands.
    - Diversity: quota q per (32 px, 6 px/t, jumps, loaded, shot in flight) cell, then fill; fine dedup by
      (pos/8, vel/2, hook, jumps, reload/4, pending explosion). Without the fill, a single-cell start keeps
      only q states and the search starves.
    - Options also used: rlw (tie-break for earlier reload), ghoste/ehgt/ecap (capped energy credit incl.
      height; +1-2 ticks), gate=col|under|gap|room|x>X|y<Y with gatevw (speed credit at the gate),
      pre=FILE (continue after given inputs), track=W (time-indexed Teero tracking).
    Typical: ./fbeam ../AiP-Gores.map beam=2000 deathpen=5 rlw=0.05 tau=6 ghoste=0.01 ehgt=1 ecap=32
      maxticks=200 out=runs/X     (entry keys: tp=x,y,vx,vy tpk=K reload=N air=0|1 prefix=FILE)
      ~2.5 s/step at beam 2000 (single thread), ~6.5 min to the finish.
6.4 What did not help: bigger beam (3000/6000: same results), longer rollouts (tau 10-16), finer hook aims,
    model=2/3, staged gates with speed credits (2572), Teero tracking (2558), speed credit by D-rate (worse).
6.5 Suggestions for seg / the main pipeline:
    1. Fix the tp clock (6.1) before trusting any tp benchmark with gate=finish.
    2. Add positional diversity (quota + fill) - the single change that makes seg survive here.
    3. Judge every child whose grenade is still in flight after its explosion (seg's pendlook does this; keep
       it on) and prefer the earlier reload on ties (pre-fire = same kick, reload back earlier).
    4. In maze sections use a geodesic-distance progress (distfield.h with goal = finish/gate tiles) and
       short rollouts with a death penalty instead of an energy/speed credit; energy credit only capped
      (|v| <= ~32) - beyond that speed must be destroyed before the next turn anyway.
    5. The search is locally at par with Teero; the remaining loss is long-horizon (reload phase and energy
       through the up-turn decide the top turn ~25 ticks later). A value that knows the next kick's timing
       (e.g. rollouts that may kick when loaded, not only to avoid death) is the most promising next step.

=========================================================================================================
7. TOOLS (physlab4; nothing outside modified; all single-threaded)
=========================================================================================================
- flcommon.h: entry construction (prefix replay, teleport, hook idle, jump state, reload, race clock with the
  4000-tick world-clock shift), input I/O, explosion listing, distance field.
- fl (fl.cpp): `fl MAP trace in=FILE [entry]` per-tick states (FILE lines "d j h f deg [n]" or raw 7-field
  lines); `fl MAP df` geodesic D along Teero's track; `AT=i LEN=n STEP=deg fl MAP scanfire in=FILE` fire every
  aim on input i and continue the file's inputs; `fl MAP bench`.
- fbeam (fbeam.cpp): the beam search above. Writes OUT.txt (post-entry inputs) and OUT.trace.
- fhc (fhc.cpp): (1+1) hill climbing on a post-entry input file with the exact simulator (dir runs, jump
  toggles, hook press/release shifts, hook/fire aim nudges, shot moves/adds/removes); objective finish tick
  + sub-tick, or a gate (col/under/gap/room/x>X/y<Y) with vw x speed credit. ~25k evaluations/s.
- segx/seg.cpp: seg with the tp clock fix and quota=Q (sec. 6.2).
- scripts: teerovel.py (Teero velocity from the track), splits.py (gate ticks), lag.py (D-lag vs Teero),
  cmp.py (tick-by-tick vs Teero), ov.py (map overlay of a trace), stages.sh, lns.sh, runs/*.sh.
- Replay a result: ./fl ../AiP-Gores.map trace in=results/entry2390_best.txt tp=7817,4000,60.7,21.6 tpk=2390
  reload=3 air=1        (prefix ../runs/ex/e1025.txt is the default; the finish shows as "fin 2552").
