2. WHAT LIMITS THE HAIRPIN (verified mechanics)
=========================================================================================================
M1. Geometry. The shaft is x 9344-9472 (tee centre), 4 tiles wide, freeze on both sides. The solid faces behind
    the freeze are at x 9312 (left) and x 9472 (row 58) / 9504 (rows 59-62) / 9536 (rows 63-64). The only bare
    surfaces are the L-block: top y 2144 for x 9344-9504, left end face x 9344, vertical arm left face x 9472 at
    y 2080-2144. Corridor B has a freeze floor (face y 2208, solid y 2240, x 8480-9024). Its ceiling is freeze at
    y 2112 (solid y 2080) for x 7936-8416, and freeze row 64 (y 2048-2080) for x 7616-7968, just above the column.
    Between x 9056 and 9344 there is a pit under the exit, down to y ~2300.
M2. Braking. We arrive at 54 px/t (Teero ~50). A hook toward the anchor plus air control gives at most
    ~4.3-5 px/t^2 (above vx ~30, dir 0 with the hook beats dir -1, because the 5% air friction is larger than
    1.5). So 11-12 ticks and ~230 px of braking: the base brakes over rt 1143-1157. All incoming kinetic energy is
    lost (E from +1200 to -1400).
M3. Turn-radius limit. A hook turns velocity at most at a = 3 px/t^2 (x x0.95/0.75, down x0.3), so
    R = v^2/3. The shaft plus the exit leave R ~200-250 px, so the swing runs at <= 25-29 px/t. The exit speed
    after a pure hook swing is sqrt(v_top^2 + dy) with dy ~240 (the base: 24.7 -> 28.0, against a bound of 29.4).
    A kick that makes the fall faster (down-kick in the shaft) cannot be turned in time.
M4. Kick efficiency. d(v^2) = 2*12*|v|*cos(theta) + 144. In the shaft the velocity points down, but the walls
    give horizontal kicks, so cos ~0.1-0.5 and the gain is only +3..6.5 px/t (base K5 at rt 1158: 18.2 -> 24.7,
    E +279). At the corner, with v ~(-25,12), a point-blank shot on the L-block top just right of its left end
    kicks (-0.87,-0.5): cos ~0.9, +10 px/t. That is Teero's "K6": energy from the track below, k1164-1168,
    |v| 28 -> 38 (E -1331 -> -710). Corridor-B floor kicks give at most (-8.5,-8.5): the tee at y >= 2195, aim
    45 deg down-right.
M5. The reload clock. In our frame, kick slots are: floor block 1122-1125, shaft-top right wall 1157-1160,
    L-block corner 1167-1171, corridor-B floor 1181-1205 (x <= 9000 and low), column top ~1220. Our 1100 shot
    puts the reload at 1125, and the 1125 floor kick then gives >= 1150 and >= 1175. The corner slot falls
    between them. A point-blank at 1175 is 6 ticks too late: the tee is at x ~9160 with no surface within 100 px.
    Without the 1158 shot the base tee freezes at rt 1159 (x 9441 in the row 58-59 freeze), so that shot is not
    "useless". It is K5: +6.5 px/t, and it steers the tee off the right wall.
M6. Pre-fire timing law. The grenade flies 20 px/t and we approach at 37-54 px/t, so T_E = t_fire + dist/20 with
    dist shrinking faster than t_fire grows. A later shot explodes earlier. From the corridor (fired 1150-1156),
    every right-wall or L-block explosion lands at rt 1162-1168. Shots fired <= 1146 from x <= 9060 need 23-28
    ticks of flight (T_E 1166-1172). The L-block and corner region can be reached from the corridor only through
    the shaft opening past the solid corner (9312,1888), i.e. from x >= ~9250 (rt >= ~1150). A same-tick double
    kick at the corner needs >= 25 ticks of flight, which is impossible (max ~20).
M7. With the pre-fired K5 (prefireK5), the reload is free at 1175, but corridor B offers no leftward kick before
    ~1181. The tee moves left with the floor below and behind it, and the pit's diagonal edge pushes right.
    Forced floor kicks at 1180/1182 gave 1226.6-1228.9 (the tee is not low enough yet).
M8. Energy origin of our deficit, before the cut. At rt 1097 the base is at v (37.9,-22.8) and at rt 1098 it hits
    the underside of block (235-236,43): vy goes to 0 and E drops from 513 to 14. The rt 1100 point-blank on that
    block then adds (+3.8,+12.4). Teero kicks off the same block (#4, k1095.8 = our 1100.8) without bumping it, so
    his approach E is ~800-990 against our ~450 at rt 1100-1116 (|v| 50 against 45). Our 1125 floor kick
    (E 151 -> 1232) compensates, but it costs the reload phase. Without it (Option Y from rt 1124), E = 125: the tee
    is ~2 ticks later at x 9000, and the pre-fire from x 8930-9040 explodes at 1166-1172. The tee then has to
    crawl (vx ~20 at rt 1148) to rendezvous.

=========================================================================================================
5. TOOLS (physlab3, single-threaded; build: ./build.sh NAME)
=========================================================================================================
- hp (hp.cpp): beam to a gate (x <= gatex, vx <= -gatevx, gatey0 <= y <= gatey) with value modes:
  mode=1 (Teero ghost: rt + K_gate - k(s) - tT + H/v_along, chord=1 aims along the chord H px ahead, capk caps
  the speed before the turn), 2/4 (exit energy), 5 (Teero tracking at lag lagt), 6 (rendezvous for a forced
  pre-fire). forcetgt=RT:X,Y fires at RT with each node's own aim whose explosion lands nearest to (X,Y)
  (AimForTarget: +-15 deg by 0.5, then 0.05). Other options: forcefire=RT:DEG, firefrom/fireto/fire2 shot windows,
  minper/maxkeys (reserved slots per pending-explosion key), survival fallback. ~20-90 s per run at beam 2000.
- hcx (hcx.cpp): (1+lambda) hill climbing over per-tick genes (dir, jump, hook, fire, aim; seed aims kept
  exactly, mutated aims written with radius 10000) with the exact fractional gate time and a full-simulation
  survival check (FullSurvives: dir -1/0 x {no hook, hook 45/90/135/160/180 deg}). ~5000 evals/s.
- gates.py FILE..: the milestone table above (x <= 9100/8700/8200/7700). alive.sh FILE..: continuation check.
  ex.py: base plus modifications through xlab. xlab, xl.py, srvtrace.sh, killpy.sh: copies from physlab2.

6. SUGGESTIONS FOR seg
=========================================================================================================
S1. Gates. A corridor-B gate must test y (2040 <= y <= 2098 at x <= 7700). "x <= 7700" alone accepts lines
    at y ~2197 that hit the column's right face at x 7694 (e1025v1 "reaches" 1224 that way). A hill climber also
    found x <= 7700 at rt 1209 at y 1666 by turning back in the upper corridor. Check survival with full-sim
    rollouts: core-only Rollout passed doomed states under the freeze row 64 above the column (all children
    froze 2 ticks later).
S2. Pre-fires. (a) Aim to a target point, not at fixed angles. The shot starts from the position before the
    step, independent of that step's dir/hook, so one aim scan per node is enough. (b) Rank nodes with a pending
    explosion by a rendezvous value, not by the normal value: the miss of the ballistic prediction at T_E-1 minus
    the reachable radius 0.5*3.5*Rem^2, plus a velocity term. (c) Give the pending pool its own share of the beam
    until T_E; otherwise nodes whose shot already exploded crowd them out. With these, hp reproduced the K5 kick
    from a pre-fire (prefireK5) and the rendezvous at the right wall (rvt) in ~20 s. Plain kick-potential credits
    (seg pendshare, my kg/kdecay) did not.
S3. Value kicks by alignment, d(v^2) = 24|v|cos + 144: corner kick ~+10, shaft-top wall kick +3..6.5,
    corridor-B floor <= 8.5 in x. Credit reload readiness only where an aligned surface lies ahead.
S4. Plan the shot schedule before the beam. Enumerate the kick slots of M5 under the 25-tick reload and try
    each schedule as a constraint (forced fire windows). The present search cannot see that the 1125 floor kick
    forbids the corner slot.
S5. The real lever is upstream (M8). Run seg from rt ~1080 with a gate after the hairpin (x <= 8700, k1185.6),
    penalising ceiling and wall speed loss (the rt 1098 bump is -500 E). Seed it with "kick off block
    (235-236,43) at rt >= 1100 without touching it", then use Teero's schedule: no shot until a pre-fire at
    ~1144-1146, point-blank on the L-block top just right of x 9344 at ~1169-1171, floor kick at x ~8500
    (~1196), column top (~1221).
S6. Don't extend a finished seg line from its last tick: e1025v1 at rt 1208 (y 2112, under the freeze ceiling)
    can no longer clear the column. Extending from rt 1195 gives 1223.5.
