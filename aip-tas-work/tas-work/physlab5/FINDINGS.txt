=========================================================================================================
1. THE NEW PREFIX HAS NO AIR JUMP. RE-LANDING ON THE BLOCK
=========================================================================================================
- kog_pregren_975_fast never touches ground after rt 839, so its air jump is spent (jumped=2) at the pickup.
  The braked pickup needs that jump: it sets vy from +23 to -12 instantly at the pickup disc. A hook alone takes
  ~14 ticks to do the same. The old 978 run refilled the jump by touching the block top at rt 970: row 72,
  x 5184-5279, top y=2304.
- Grounding while keeping the downward speed works like this. At the start of a tick, the tee must be at
  x<=5293 and y in [2285,2290). Then the move must slide past the block corner. With v ~(22,23), that works
  for x ~5290-5293. If x is too far left, the tee lands and vy is set to 0. That costs about 15 ticks and runs
  into the freeze at (5376-5407, 2304-2335).
- s1_land.py (random mutation from cuts at rt 935/945/955): the earliest grounding with vy kept is rt 968, at
  (5293,2289) with v (22.4,23.4). The old run grounded at 970. Rt 967 was never found: the prefix passes
  (5289,2283) at 967, 2 px too high. Turning vx into vy with down-hooks costs too much x.
  Other contacts found (the pillar top at x~5030, rt 958; the upper block row 71, rt 966-967) are much slower.
- Transplanting the old T1025 inputs at the aligned tick does not work directly: it either lands with vy=0 or
  misses the ground.

2. TURNAROUND AND EXIT
=========================================================================================================
- b5_turn.py hill-climbs rt 956-1000 from the rt-955 cut. The gate is y<=2290, x<=5376, grenade active,
  reload 0, vy<=-12, and alive for 6 more ticks. The gate time is scored fractionally (env YFRAC=1): the time
  at which y crosses 2290.
  The integer gate time with a y tie-break got stuck at 992. The fractional score reaches 990.79, with y 2287 at
  rt 991 (runs/b5_955_2290_7_v3f7.json). Cuts at 940 and 945 give the same 990.8, so it looks converged.
  The line (E1017):
    - 967-969: right hook (999,45) and land on the block at 968.
    - 972-977: up-left hook (-821,-571) on the block face, dir -1 from 975. vx goes 22 -> 3 and vy 25 -> 15.
    - 978: air jump plus pickup, at (5409,2466).
    - 981-989: hook climb past the overhang corner.
- Pre-fire timing rule (gmodel M6, confirmed). Only face hits with n >= 26 are usable, because the point-blank
  needs the reload back (pre-fire step + 25). So the exit tick e >= m + 25, where m is the pre-fire step, and
  n = 26 needs a fire height of y <= ~2290 at S[m-1].
  With y 2287 at rt 991, the pre-fire goes on step 992: aim (-220,-976), 257.3 deg, n=26. The explosion is at
  rt 1017, which is the earliest exit possible for this turnaround. One more tick would need y<=2290 at rt 990
  (now 2302).
- The climb to the face (xrv beam, then p7.py polish) runs at 13-14 px/t. Right-wall hook pre-acceleration
  over 1013-1016 takes vx from -2.8 to 8.65 at x 5295. The tee center can't go left of x~5280 (freeze
  column 164), and it must be within ~48 px of the explosion, which caps vx before the kick at ~9-10.
- Exit patterns. "Same step" here means both grenades explode in the same simulation step.
    A. Fire the point-blank on the explosion step e from x<=~5289 (within ~41 px along the aim). It explodes in
       its first segment.
    B. Fire the point-blank on step e-1 from 42-61 px. It explodes in its 2nd segment at step e, together with
       the pre-fire. T1025 and E1018B do this: +23.0. Pattern B needs n >= 27, because of the reload.
    Hybrid (E1017): fire on step e from x 5295. The pre-fire kicks at e (+12.0) and the point-blank at e+1
       (+9.4, since the tee has already moved 19 px away).
  Hybrid at e=1017 and pattern B at e=1018 end up 0.2 tick apart at x=5671. Pattern A gives the same position
  at e+1 as the hybrid.

=========================================================================================================
3. SMALL THINGS LEARNED
=========================================================================================================
- Velocity ramp: at |v| ~31, x moves ~0.83-0.85*vx per tick. Lowering |v| raises the x displacement for the
  same vx.
  After the exit |v| > 15, so a hook only acts when it reduces |v|. A short down-right hook that eats some of
  the upward vy (E1017, rt 1018-1021, grabs at 1020: vy -9.05 -> -7.65, vx 30.02 -> 30.25) shrinks |v| and the
  ramp penalty. Result: x 5675 instead of 5667 at rt 1031.
  The clean dir-1 tail reaches x>=5671 at 1031.15, i.e. 1032. The result files keep the optimized tail up to
  x>=5671, then dir 1 only.
- xlab's Step command sends weapon -1 until the grenade is held. The files send weapon 3 on every new line.
  Both behave the same, because nothing fires at pickup+1. ev.py, metrics.py, table.py and cmpsrv.py replay
  the exact file lines.
- Downstream coupling: in both files the point-blank fires at rt 1017, so the reload is back at step 1042.
  With vx 30-31.5 the block (x 5888) is reached at ~1039, ~3 ticks before the reload. Teero has 0 ticks
  of slack, and the old lines had 4-6.
  A block kick at ~1042 compares with rt 1050 in the old T1025/c3s3 line. The downstream search should cut at
  the exit (rt 1017/1018) and plan the block landing (jump refill) and the kick at the reload.
